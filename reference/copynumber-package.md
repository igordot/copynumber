# copynumber: Segmentation of single- and multi-track copy number data by penalized least squares regression (with hg38 and mm10).

Penalized least squares regression is applied to fit piecewise constant
curves to copy number data to locate genomic regions of constant copy
number. Procedures are available for individual segmentation of each
sample, joint segmentation of several samples and joint segmentation of
the two data tracks from SNP-arrays. Several plotting functions are
available for visualization of the data and the segmentation results.
